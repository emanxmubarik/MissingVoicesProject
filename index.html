The Missing People Project

Mapping overlooked cases. Amplifying stories. Demanding accountability.

Our Mission

The Missing People Project is dedicated to documenting and mapping cases of missing individuals— especially those that receive limited media attention. This platform aims to bring visibility to overlooked stories and uncover patterns that demand accountability.

Why This Matters

Many missing persons cases—particularly involving marginalized communities—are underreported and forgotten. This project ensures that these individuals are not ignored, and that their stories are seen, shared, and remembered.
